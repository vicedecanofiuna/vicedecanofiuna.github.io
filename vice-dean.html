Next administration

Fundamental commitments.

Principles to guide an independent, transparent and results-oriented administration.

Institutional Autonomy

Guarantee the full independence of academic and administrative management, protecting the Faculty from external political-party interference.

Academic Excellence

Implement measurable, concrete strategies to improve student retention and optimize time to graduation.

Transparency and Democracy

Modernize participation mechanisms for the three constituencies and guarantee transparent access to institutional information.

Valuing Faculty

Ensure regularity, transparency and meritocracy in all faculty selection processes.

Connection with Society

Strengthen and expand active engagement with graduates and the public and private sectors.

Strategic Planning

Lay the foundations for the FIUNA we want toward the bicentennial through a clear and inclusive roadmap.
